About RoboReady

RoboReady helps robotics teams stay organized and prepared for tournaments. Never forget an essential item or miss an important deadline again!

Features

  • Tournament Management - Track all your upcoming tournaments in one place
  • Smart Checklists - Pre-built checklists for week-before and tournament-day preparation
  • Calendar Integration - Sync tournaments directly to your device calendar
  • Progress Tracking - Monitor completion status for each checklist
  • Offline Access - All data stored locally on your device
