# Privacy Policy for RoboReady

**Last Updated:** August 24, 2025

## Overview
RoboReady is a VEX Robotics tournament management app that helps teams stay organized and prepared for competitions. **We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data.**

## Data Collection
**We do not collect any personal information or user data.** All data you enter in the app stays exclusively on your device. This includes:
- **Tournament details**: Names, dates, times, and locations you enter
- **Checklist items**: Custom checklist items you create
- **Progress data**: Your checklist completion status

None of this information is transmitted to us or any third party.

## Data Storage and Security
- **Local Storage Only**: All data is stored exclusively on your device
- **No Cloud Storage**: We do not upload, transmit, or store your data on any external servers
- **Complete Privacy**: Your data never leaves your device and we have no access to it
- **Secure Storage**: Data is stored using secure local storage methods provided by your device

## Permissions
RoboReady may request the following **optional** permissions:
- **Calendar Access (Optional)**: Only if you choose to add tournament events to your device calendar. You can use the app without granting this permission.

These permissions are used only for functionality on your device. No data is transmitted to us.

## Data Sharing
- **No Third Parties**: We do not share your data with any third parties
- **No Advertising**: We do not use your data for advertising purposes

## Your Rights
You have the right to:
- **Access**: View all data stored by the app
- **Delete**: Remove all data by uninstalling the app
- **Control**: Grant or revoke permissions at any time through device settings

## Children's Privacy
RoboReady is designed for VEX Robotics teams and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Contact Information

**Email:** roboready.io@gmail.com

## Consent
By using RoboReady, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this privacy policy.
