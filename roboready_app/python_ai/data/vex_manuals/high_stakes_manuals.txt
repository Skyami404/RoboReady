# VEX High Stakes - Game Manual Summary

## Game Overview
High Stakes is a VEX V5 Robotics Competition game. Teams design, build, and program robots to score rings on mobile goals, wall stakes, and the alliance stake.

## Field Layout
- 12' x 12' field with foam tiles
- 4 mobile goals (2 red, 2 blue)
- 4 wall stakes (2 per alliance)
- 1 alliance stake (center)
- 44 rings total (22 red, 22 blue)

## Match Format
- Total match time: 2 minutes
- Autonomous period: 15 seconds
- Driver control period: 1 minute 45 seconds
- Teams compete in 2v2 alliance matches

## Scoring
### Ring Scoring:
- Rings on mobile goals:
  - Lower stake: 3 points
  - Middle stake: 4 points
  - Upper stake: 5 points

- Rings on wall stakes:
  - Lower stake: 3 points
  - Middle stake: 4 points
  - Upper stake: 5 points

- Rings on alliance stake: 6 points each

### Autonomous Bonus:
- Each preloaded ring scored during autonomous: +3 points bonus
- Autonomous Win Point (AWP): 1 point if win autonomous

### Elevation Bonus:
- Robot touching ladder at end of match
- Level 1: 5 points
- Level 2: 10 points
- Level 3: 20 points

## Robot Constraints
- Maximum starting size: 18" x 18" x 18"
- Can expand during match
- Must pass inspection before competing
- One robot per team

## Strategy Tips
- Focus on consistent ring placement
- Alliance stake is high value but limited space
- Autonomous bonus is crucial for rankings
- Practice mobile goal control
- Consider elevation for tie-breakers

## Tournament Structure
- Qualification matches (4-6 per team)
- Ranking by Win Points, AWP, Strength of Schedule
- Alliance selection (top teams choose partners)
- Elimination rounds (quarterfinals, semifinals, finals)
- Best-of-3 format for eliminations

## Skills Challenge
- Driver Skills: 1 minute manual control
- Programming Skills: 1 minute autonomous
- Combined score for global rankings
- Can attempt multiple times per tournament
