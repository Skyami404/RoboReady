# VEX Tournament Guide

## Before the Tournament

### Registration
- Register on RobotEvents.com
- Pay entry fees (if applicable)
- Note registration deadline
- Confirm team roster

### Preparation Checklist (1 Week Before)
- [ ] Robot passes inspection (size, safety)
- [ ] Autonomous routines tested and working
- [ ] Driver skills practiced
- [ ] Engineering notebook complete and organized
- [ ] Spare parts and tools packed
- [ ] Batteries fully charged
- [ ] Team roles assigned (driver, scout, pit crew)
- [ ] Review game manual one more time

### What to Bring

**Robot Equipment:**
- Robot (obviously!)
- Controller(s)
- Charged batteries (bring ALL of them)
- Battery charger
- Spare parts (gears, wheels, screws, motors)
- Tools (screwdrivers, Allen wrenches, pliers)
- Laptop with programming software
- V5 Brain USB cable
- Field elements for testing (if possible)

**Team Materials:**
- Engineering notebook
- Team information sheet
- Pit display/poster (optional but recommended)
- Team contact information
- Pens, paper, clipboard

**Personal Items:**
- Water bottles and snacks
- Comfortable shoes (you'll be standing a lot)
- Team t-shirts/uniforms
- Phone chargers
- Money for food/merchandise

## Tournament Day Schedule

### Typical Timeline

**8:00 AM - Arrive and Check In**
- Team check-in at registration
- Receive match schedule
- Get tournament information

**8:30 AM - Setup Pit Area**
- Find assigned pit space
- Unpack and organize equipment
- Set up team display
- Begin pre-tournament preparations

**9:00 AM - Opening Ceremony**
- Welcome and announcements
- Game overview
- Tournament format explanation
- Safety briefing

**9:30 AM - Robot Inspection**
- Wait for inspection slot
- Bring robot, notebook, team info
- Inspection checklist verification
- Get approval sticker

**10:00 AM - Practice Matches** (if available)
- Test robot on official field
- Verify autonomous works
- Check control responsiveness
- Make last-minute adjustments

**10:30 AM - Qualification Matches Begin**
- 4-6 matches per team
- Random alliance partners
- 5-10 minute breaks between matches
- Skills challenges throughout

**12:30 PM - Lunch Break**
- Time varies by tournament
- Usually 30-60 minutes
- Matches may continue or pause

**2:00 PM - Qualification Matches Continue**
- Complete all qualification rounds
- Rankings calculated and posted
- Prepare for eliminations

**3:30 PM - Alliance Selection**
- Top teams choose partners
- Strategy discussions
- Alliance practice time

**4:00 PM - Elimination Matches**
- Single-elimination bracket
- Best-of-3 matches
- Quarterfinals → Semifinals → Finals

**5:30 PM - Awards Ceremony**
- Excellence, Design, and other awards
- Tournament Champions crowned
- Closing remarks

**6:00 PM - Tournament Ends**
- Pack up equipment
- Clean pit area
- Head home (tired but happy!)

## Tournament Format Explained

### Qualification Matches

**Purpose:** Rank all teams fairly

**How it works:**
- Each team plays 4-6 matches
- Random alliance partners each match
- Alliances change every match
- Earn Win Points (WP) and Autonomous Win Points (AWP)

**Scoring:**
- Win: 2 WP
- Tie: 1 WP  
- Loss: 0 WP
- Autonomous Win: 1 AWP

**Rankings:** Determined by:
1. Win Points (WP) - highest
2. Autonomous Win Points (AWP) - highest
3. Strength of Schedule (SOS) - highest
4. Total Points Scored - highest

### Alliance Selection

**Eligibility:**
- Top 8-16 teams (varies by tournament size)
- Based on qualification ranking

**Process:**
1. #1 seed chooses first partner
2. #2 seed chooses first partner
3. Continue through all alliance captains
4. Second round (if needed)
5. Selected teams can accept or decline

**Strategy Tips:**
- Choose complementary robots (strengths/weaknesses)
- Consider reliability over pure performance
- Communication is key
- Have backup choices ready

### Elimination Matches

**Format:**
- Single elimination bracket
- Best-of-3 matches
- Alliances stay together
- Win 2 matches to advance

**Bracket Structure:**
- Quarterfinals (8 alliances → 4)
- Semifinals (4 alliances → 2)
- Finals (2 alliances → 1 winner)

**Higher seed advantages:**
- Choose starting position
- In case of tie in series, higher seed wins

## Skills Challenge

**Two Types:**

**Driver Skills:**
- 60 seconds manual control
- Only your robot on field
- Maximize score
- Can attempt multiple times

**Programming Skills (Autonomous):**
- 60 seconds fully autonomous
- No driver input allowed
- Pre-programmed routine
- Can attempt multiple times

**Importance:**
- Separate competition within tournament
- Global rankings (vs just tournament)
- Can qualify for championships through skills
- Best score counts (not average)

**Strategy:**
- Practice dedicated skills routines
- Different from match autonomous
- Focus on consistency
- Attempt early and often

## At the Tournament

### Queuing for Matches

**When to Queue:**
- 2-3 matches before yours
- Listen for announcements
- Check match schedule display

**Queue Area:**
- Bring robot and controller
- Have drivers ready
- Check battery level
- Last-minute checks

**On the Field:**
- Follow referee instructions
- Place robot in starting position
- Verify controller connection
- Wait for match start

### Pit Etiquette

**Your Pit Space:**
- Keep organized and tidy
- Don't spread into neighboring pits
- Secure loose parts
- No loud music/disruptions

**Safety:**
- No running in pit area
- Announce when testing robot
- Clear area for robot tests
- Follow all facility rules

**Interactions:**
- Welcome visitors to your pit
- Share knowledge with other teams
- Don't reveal strategies if uncomfortable
- Network and make friends!

### Scouting

**Why Scout:**
- Identify strong alliance partners
- Understand opponent strategies
- Plan match strategies
- Make informed alliance selections

**What to Track:**
- Robot capabilities (speed, scoring methods)
- Autonomous performance
- Reliability/consistency
- Driver skill level
- Team communication

**Scouting Tools:**
- Paper forms or digital apps
- Note key strengths/weaknesses
- Record match outcomes
- Share data with team

## Interview with Judges

**What to Expect:**
- 5-10 minute conversation
- Questions about robot and notebook
- Opportunity to showcase your work
- Informal and friendly

**Prepare to Discuss:**
- Design process and decisions
- Challenges faced and solutions
- Team roles and collaboration
- What makes your robot unique
- Engineering notebook highlights

**Tips:**
- Be enthusiastic and confident
- Speak clearly and honestly
- Let all team members participate
- Bring your notebook
- Practice beforehand

## Common Issues and Solutions

**Robot Won't Connect:**
- Check battery level
- Verify V5 Brain is on
- Re-pair controller
- Restart both devices
- Check VEXnet keys

**Last-Minute Breakage:**
- Use spare parts
- Simplify design if needed
- Ask other teams for help
- Have backup plan

**Autonomous Not Working:**
- Test on practice field
- Check battery level (affects speed)
- Verify sensor calibration
- Have simplified backup routine

**Forgot Something:**
- Ask other teams (they usually help!)
- Tournament organizers may have supplies
- Improvise if possible
- Learn for next time

## After Your Match

**Win or Lose:**
- Shake hands with opponents
- Thank alliance partner
- Review match performance
- Note what to improve

**Between Matches:**
- Charge batteries
- Make necessary repairs
- Review upcoming match schedule
- Rest and hydrate

**Document:**
- Take photos/videos
- Update engineering notebook
- Record match scores
- Note lessons learned

## Tournament Survival Tips

1. **Arrive Early:** Avoid stress, have time to prepare
2. **Stay Hydrated:** Bring water, drink regularly
3. **Eat Well:** Tournament days are long
4. **Take Breaks:** Rest when possible
5. **Stay Positive:** Support teammates through ups and downs
6. **Ask Questions:** Officials and volunteers are there to help
7. **Make Friends:** Network with other teams
8. **Have Fun:** Remember why you joined robotics!

## After the Tournament

**Debrief:**
- Discuss what went well
- Identify areas for improvement
- Celebrate successes
- Plan for next tournament

**Maintenance:**
- Inspect robot for damage
- Recharge all batteries
- Organize and inventory parts
- Store safely until next event

**Follow Up:**
- Thank mentors and volunteers
- Post on social media (if allowed)
- Update engineering notebook
- Start preparing for next competition

## Championship Qualification

**Paths to Championships:**
- Excellence Award at qualifying event
- Tournament Champions at qualifying event
- High skills ranking globally
- Wildcard invitations (limited)

**Regional/State/Nationals:**
- Levels vary by location
- Build on previous successes
- Increasing competition level
- Ultimate goal: VEX Worlds
